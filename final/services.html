How It Works Three steps, ninety seconds

The longest part is choosing. The oven does the rest.

  1. Choose

    A size, your toppings, takeaway or a table. The order page adds it up as you click.

  2. We bake

    Stretched by hand, fired at 450 °C for ninety seconds, sliced and boxed or plated.

  3. Enjoy

    Collect it at the counter or have it at your table, still blistering.
