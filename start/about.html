About Dough, Fire and Patience

One oven, a dough that rests for two days, and five toppings we trust. We keep the menu short so every pizza that leaves the pass is one we are proud of.

A Short Menu On purpose

We opened with one wood-fired oven and a list of ingredients we could buy fresh every morning. Three sizes, five toppings, no surprises. Ten years on, the list has not grown, and that is the point.

The dough is flour, water, salt and a little yeast, left to rest for forty-eight hours. It is stretched by hand, never rolled, so the rim keeps its air and blisters in the heat.

Ask for something that is not on the menu and we will say no, kindly. Pineapple included.

What We Hold To Three rules at the pass

They are written on the wall of the kitchen, above the oven, where nobody can miss them.

01

Slow dough

Two days of rest, every day. Rushed dough tastes of flour; ours tastes of bread.

02

Fresh, then fast

Everything is prepared in the morning and cooked in ninety seconds. Nothing waits under a lamp.

03

Nothing off the menu

A closed list is a promise: whatever you order, we have made it a thousand times.
